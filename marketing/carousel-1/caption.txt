we made a tiny world we both live in 🫙

Little Us is a cozy game for two. You share one little jar from your own phones, so whatever one of you plants, builds or finds, the other sees too.

✿ one question a day, sealed until you've both answered
✿ a little mission every week, together or over a call
✿ fishing, bugs, a garden, a house to fill, a museum, 8 jars to explore

send this to the person you'd share a jar with 💗
